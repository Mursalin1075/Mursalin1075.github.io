About

I'm an electrical and electronic engineer based in Melbourne. I graduated from Swinburne University of Technology with a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) with Distinction, and I'm now a PhD candidate at Deakin University through the REACH Trailblazer Program.

My research is on multi-port power converters for apartment-scale solar and battery systems: compact, efficient hardware that serves AC loads and modern USB-C devices from one unit. Alongside it I build embedded and IoT products as an R&D Engineer at ASA Industry and teach renewable energy at Swinburne.

Longer term, I want to help build energy infrastructure in Bangladesh, where reliable power still changes lives.
